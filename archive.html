    Contemporary note

    Bernoulli–IMS One World Symposium

    2020 · Computational Statistics

    Bernoulli–IMS symposium became an online event this year. As the chair of Computational Statistics, I would like to thank Tony Lelièvre, Samuel Livingstone, and Lyudmila Sakhanenko for moderating the session.

    Despite the difficulties holding this kind of new style of conference, it was quite smooth and successful. I enjoyed the discussion at Coffee garden using gather.town.

    I would like to thank the excellent work by the organizer chair Leif Döring. Also, I would like to thank Christian P. Robert for inviting me to the event.
